# Audio foundation stability — 2026-10-09

Scope: W9.1 packet/codec/reorder foundation and W0.1 regression evidence.
No live audio service, device I/O, network playout clock or A/V synchronization
is implemented by this increment. See the [current contract](../audio.md).

## Reproduced defects

Seven integration tests failed against the original `93be1f0` implementation:

- malformed framing, a mislabeled compressed duration and a multi-frame packet
  could pass wire/buffer admission;
- a maximum-size single frame plus TOC was refused by the 1275-byte packet cap;
- refusing a wrong-duration packet after decode already changed decoder history;
- an older out-of-order arrival could evict a newer retained packet;
- `start_at` retained packets below its newly declared playout floor;
- multiple missing frames produced only one PLC interval;
- sequence exhaustion could wrap and admit old audio again.

Admission now shares libopus parsing/duration validation before buffer mutation
or stateful decode. The one-frame packet bound is 1276 bytes, and valid coded
mono remains compatible with stereo PCM output. Jitter retains the newest
bounded packets, reports individual small gaps or one explicit large
discontinuity, and refuses sequence wrap. Counters saturate. No unsafe block,
third-party dependency, authorization change or remote wire revision was added.

The [release benchmark](bench-w9-audio-foundation.md) is generated by
`rds-bench/examples/audio_foundation.rs`; [raw JSON](bench-w9-audio-foundation.json)
binds clean source/tree, binary and lockfile digests, toolchain, features and
sample counts. Across ten rate/channel combinations, it encodes 5,000 frames,
decodes 4,900 and conceals 100 deliberately omitted frames with exact output
duration. Retention never exceeds four packets. It verifies finite PCM and
sequence accounting, not perceptual audio quality or installed-device latency.

## Manual review

All seven original failure classes now have passing regressions. Additional
tests cover every supported PCM format, malformed admission preserving queued
audio, all permutations of four arrivals, a huge sequence jump and saturated
counters. Large gaps have explicit caller-visible reset semantics; no clock or
device readiness claim is inferred from them. The original generated checkpoint
and its receipt are retained in Git before this reviewed checklist update.

Native device capture/playback, microphone permission, clock drift, A/V timing,
fairness with other services and installed-media qualification remain open.
This closes only the registered audio-foundation increment.
